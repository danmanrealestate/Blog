Commercial Investor Academy — Lesson 40: CAM charges often shift operating costs to tenants through monthly estimates and annual reconciliations. For newer investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties, understanding CAM definitions, exclusions, and reconciliation mechanics protects net operating income and tenant relationships. Practical steps: review lease language for explicit CAM inclusions and exclusions, confirm allocation method (square footage versus pro rata), verify audit rights, and insist on line-item vendor invoices during annual reconciliations. Watch red flags: capital projects absorbed as CAM, duplicated management fees, vague common-area definitions, or sudden unexplained expense spikes. Locally, include municipal services such as snow removal and stormwater charges when assessing CAM, and budget for seasonal cost variability.
