CAM (Common Area Maintenance) charges and annual reconciliations routinely change a property's cash flow and tenant obligations. For newer CRE investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester & Montgomery Counties, PA, knowing how to read CAM bills is essential. Quick checklist: request three years of CAM reconciliations and vendor contracts; verify line-item backups and whether capital expenses were wrongly passed through; inspect property services like snow removal and HVAC; confirm allocation method and any caps in the lease; budget for the annual true-up. Local note: seasonal costs (winter snow removal, summer landscaping) materially affect year-end adjustments here — ask about historical timing and vendor pricing.
