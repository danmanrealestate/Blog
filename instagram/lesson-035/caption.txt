Cash-on-cash return (CoC) is the quickest way to see how much cash your commercial property generates relative to the cash you actually put in. Calculate it by taking annual pre-tax cash flow (after debt service and realistic vacancy/reserve allowances) and dividing that by total cash invested (down payment, closing costs, initial rehab). Practical checks: always include debt service, budget for reserves and CapEx, and model a stabilization period so early-year CoC isn't misleading. Remember CoC measures short-term cash yield — pair it with total return metrics like equity growth and exit assumptions.
