Lesson 32 — How to Read a Commercial Rent Roll A rent roll is your income ledger and early-warning system for any commercial property. Start by scanning tenant names, unit IDs, listed rents, lease start and expiration dates, and occupancy status. Then verify the numbers—cross-check listed rents with bank deposits and the owner’s general ledger to confirm collected income and expose free-rent periods or undisclosed concessions. Pay attention to leases expiring in the next six to twelve months, renewal options, and escalation clauses; these shape realistic underwriting and vacancy assumptions. Evaluate tenant risk by reviewing business type, permitted use clauses, and subletting rights. For Chester and Montgomery County properties, confirm zoning compatibility and compare listed rents to local market comps before you make an offer.
