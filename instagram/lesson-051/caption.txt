Lesson 51: Commercial Property Due Diligence Checklist — a practical guide for newer investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties, PA. Due diligence means turning unknowns into documented risks before you commit capital. Start with a full physical inspection of structure, roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical and pest evidence; get contractor estimates and schedule repairs into your financial model. Order a Phase I environmental site assessment when historical use is unclear, and budget for a Phase II if sampling is needed. Obtain a current ALTA/land survey and title report to confirm lines, easements, parking counts, and access rights. Review leases, rent rolls and municipal records for zoning, permits, historical district limits, and tax assessment nuances. Build contingencies, define walk-away thresholds, and involve local inspectors and attorneys early. Need a printable checklist or local referrals?
