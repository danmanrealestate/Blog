Lesson 46 — Ten red flags before buying commercial property. This post highlights concrete checks new investors should run before committing capital: physical condition and deferred capex, lease and tenant credit scrutiny, zoning and permits, environmental screening, parking and access, and realistic market income validation. Start with a one-page due-diligence checklist, order a thorough building inspection, verify every lease against the rent roll, confirm municipal zoning and utility rules in Chester and Montgomery Counties, and budget realistic contingency dollars. Talk to local brokers, inspectors, and attorneys who know township permitting and local market quirks. When a red flag appears, quantify mitigation costs, negotiate price or holdbacks, or walk away if risk outweighs reward.
