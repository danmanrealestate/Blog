Buying commercial property feels exciting, but small oversights quickly become expensive headaches. This lesson distills ten red flags to surface early so you can protect returns and avoid surprises. Learn to spot physical deterioration, ambiguous leases, zoning limits, environmental exposure, and incomplete paperwork that change deal economics or timelines. Start practical: do a thorough site walk, order targeted inspections (roof, MEP, foundation), and collect executed leases with payment histories and amendment logs. Confirm tenant responsibilities for CAM, utilities, and repairs, and verify lease expirations and options. Check zoning, parking requirements, certificates of occupancy, and run a basic environmental screen. Triage findings into deal-killers, negotiable fixes, or post-closing responsibilities to price risk into your offer. If you own or want to buy in Chester or Montgomery Counties, carve out a 10–14 day due diligence window and involve local municipal staff early.
