Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is a core underwriting metric lenders and investors use to confirm a property can support debt. In Chester County and Montgomery County, DSCR helps you understand possible loan outcomes, set contingency reserves, and compare financing options. Improve DSCR by increasing Net Operating Income (NOI), controlling operating expenses, and choosing loan structures that align with cash flow. When modeling deals, run conservative income scenarios and include realistic vacancy and expense buffers. Remember DSCR excludes capital expenditures and owner draws—plan separately for those costs.
