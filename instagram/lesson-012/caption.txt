Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is one of the clearest, most repeatable measures you can use when underwriting commercial real estate in Chester County or Montgomery County. It strips away accounting noise and answers a basic question: does the property’s operating income cover the loan payments? Start with reliable NOI — recurring rental and ancillary income minus recurring operating expenses — then divide by the annual principal and interest payments. Use conservative vacancy and expense assumptions and separate capital expenditures from operating expenses so you don’t overstate NOI. If DSCR is tight, consider ways to improve cash flow (rent steps, expense control, value-add), reduce loan size, or negotiate terms with lenders. This lesson is meant to be evergreen: always verify actual income/expense statements, run sensitivity scenarios, and align your target DSCR with lender requirements and your risk tolerance.
