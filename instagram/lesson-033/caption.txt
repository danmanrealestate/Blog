Lesson 33 — Triple Net (NNN) Leases Explained. NNN leases shift property taxes, insurance, and common-area maintenance onto the tenant while landlords collect net rent. That structure can stabilize cash flow but creates documentation, capital, and credit risks you must manage. Use this carousel as a practical checklist when evaluating NNN deals: read exact definitions for 'taxes,' 'insurance,' and 'CAM'; inspect property condition and identify who covers roof/HVAC/foundation replacements; vet tenant credit, personal guarantees, assignment rights, and CAM reconciliation timing; confirm local tax billing cycles and seasonal maintenance costs in Chester and Montgomery Counties.
