Triple Net (NNN) leases can simplify landlord operations by shifting taxes, insurance and many operating costs to tenants — but they also change who carries what risks. This lesson gives practical checks you can apply when underwriting or negotiating NNN deals in Chester and Montgomery Counties. Key steps: read the lease definitions carefully, confirm what exactly counts as recoverable expenses, insist on audit rights and fair gross-up language, and align lease length and tenant credit with building age and deferred capital needs. Locally, check municipal tax assessment histories and any special district levies that could affect operating cost exposure. Gather these documents early: the full lease, three years of tax bills, insurance invoices, and operating expense statements.
