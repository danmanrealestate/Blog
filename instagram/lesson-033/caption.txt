Triple net (NNN) leases can simplify landlord income but shift many ongoing costs to tenants. Before you sign, read the lease line-by-line: confirm what counts as CAM, how costs are allocated and grossed up, whether capital projects or tax reassessments can be passed through, and whether caps, exclusions, or amortization schedules limit spikes. Check audit rights, reserve handling, and the allocation basis (square footage, unit, or tenant share). Verify tenant credit, guarantors, lease length, and renewal options—weak financials increase the risk of unrecoverable pass-throughs. Consider requiring security deposits, letters of credit, or parent guarantees to protect cash flow. For Chester and Montgomery County properties, also review recent assessment trends and typical local service fees that affect net expenses.
