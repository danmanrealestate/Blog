Triple net (NNN) leases pass property taxes, insurance and many operating costs to tenants — but the contract language controls the real risk. This post walks through which clauses to verify, negotiate, and audit before you commit. Key checks: precise expense definitions, CAM calculation formulas, caps or exclusions for capital items, explicit responsibility for roof/HVAC/foundation, annual reconciliations and tenant audit rights, and alignment between lease term and financing needs. Lenders focus on tenant credit and clear NNN obligations, so vague language can complicate lending or future sales. In Chester and Montgomery counties, older storefronts and mixed‑use buildings can expose ambiguous pass‑throughs or deferred maintenance.
