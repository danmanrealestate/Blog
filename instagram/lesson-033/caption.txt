Triple net (NNN) leases shift many property operating expenses to the tenant, but liability depends entirely on lease language. For newer commercial investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties, don’t accept “NNN” as shorthand—verify specifics. Request the last three years of property tax bills, insurance invoices, CAM reconciliations, and the lease’s detailed expense allocation. Confirm escalation formulas, expense caps, audit rights, who pays capital repairs, and whether the tenant’s credit supports long-term obligations.
