Triple Net (NNN) leases shift many routine operating costs from landlord to tenant. Under a true NNN the tenant pays property taxes, building insurance, common-area maintenance and day-to-day operating expenses. For investors and building owners in Chester County and Montgomery County, PA, NNN leases can simplify management, deliver predictable net cash flow, and reduce landlord involvement. They are commonly used for single-tenant retail, office, and industrial properties with creditworthy tenants and multi-year leases. Always review lease language to confirm landlord versus tenant obligations—major structural repairs, capital projects, and financing typically remain the landlord's responsibility.
