Triple Net (NNN) leases are a foundational tool in commercial real estate for transferring many operating costs from landlords to tenants. For property owners, investors, and business owners in Chester County and Montgomery County, understanding how property taxes, insurance, CAM, utilities, and routine maintenance are allocated in a lease is essential before buying, selling, or negotiating terms. NNN leases vary widely—what one lease calls a CAM charge another may exclude—so clear definitions, caps on increases, audit rights, and responsibilities for structural items should be negotiated and documented. Local considerations such as municipal tax assessments, winter maintenance needs, and utility access can influence lease language and cost flows. Before signing or listing a NNN property, review the lease schedule line-by-line, confirm who handles capital expenditures, and consult experienced local counsel or brokerage to protect value and cash flow.
