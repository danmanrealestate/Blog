Lesson 26: Evaluating Tenant Quality and Lease Strength — a practical checklist for new commercial investors, landlords, and business owners across Chester and Montgomery Counties. Why it matters: tenant credit and lease language drive income stability, re-leasing difficulty, and eventual resale value. Start with tenant fundamentals: request financials, POS or bank statements, and operating history where available. Read lease clauses closely: assignment rights, termination options, CAM/maintenance charge formulas, exclusivity, and landlord change powers. Gauge industry risk—confirm occupancy, employee counts, and remote-work policies for office tenants to assess future cash flow. Mitigate risk with lease protections: corporate or personal guarantees and security deposits sized to cover expected re-leasing and downtime. Do a local walk-through during peak hours to observe foot traffic, parking, and complementary businesses. If any element is unclear, bring in a local broker, attorney, or property manager to review lease strength and fit. Need a tenant-quality checklist or a local market review?
