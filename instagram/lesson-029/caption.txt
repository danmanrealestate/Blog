Lesson 29: Protecting NOI starts with disciplined, proactive property management. Regular lease audits, strict rent enforcement, planned maintenance, vendor oversight, and clear KPI reporting collectively prevent revenue leakage and costly emergency spending that erode returns. If you’re new to commercial investing in Chester or Montgomery Counties, insist on managers who know Pennsylvania landlord‑tenant law, maintain local emergency vendor networks, and produce transparent monthly KPI reports (occupancy, collections, repair backlog). Practical first steps: audit three random leases, request CAM reconciliations and vendor contracts, inspect HVAC and life‑safety maintenance records, and require an emergency vendor list and local references. Start by asking managers for three local references and a sample monthly KPI report; treat missing documentation as a disqualifier during vetting.
