Due diligence turns uncertainty into decisions. Before you buy, refinance, or renew a lease in Chester or Montgomery Counties, follow a structured checklist: order a qualified property inspection, get a Phase I environmental review, and obtain an ALTA/NSPS survey with a title commitment. Confirm zoning, parking rules, curb-cut permissions, stormwater controls, and utility connections with local township and authority offices. Collect contractor repair estimates for roof, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical before negotiating price or repairs. Treat information gaps as contingencies or walk‑away triggers, and tie every step to a clear timeline and your deposit protections.
