Lesson 48 — Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) DSCR measures whether a property's Net Operating Income (NOI) can cover annual debt payments. Calculate DSCR early using a conservative, normalized NOI (trailing-12 adjusted for owner-only expenses and realistic vacancy). Use it as a primary cashflow safety check and stress-test multiple downside scenarios—higher vacancy, rent pressure, or rising operating costs. Always request the lender’s DSCR worksheet and confirm how they treat debt service assumptions, escrows, interest-only periods, and recoveries; reconcile those inputs with your model. Locally in Chester and Montgomery Counties, normalize rents against recent local comps and expense trends before submitting an LOI. Want the DSCR worksheet or a quick number review?
