Lesson 17: Protecting NOI starts with strong, repeatable property management. Management isn’t just collecting rent — it’s systems: lease enforcement, rent records, CAM reconciliations, preventive maintenance, and vendor oversight. Start by auditing recent rent ledgers, confirming maintenance schedules for critical systems (HVAC, plumbing, roof), and reviewing vendor contracts for unexplained increases. Walk the property monthly with your manager, check communication logs with tenants, and maintain a modest capital reserve for predictable system renewals. Local investors should also verify winter services, stormwater plans, and tenant mix suitability for Chester and Montgomery County markets. Want a simple audit checklist to get started?
