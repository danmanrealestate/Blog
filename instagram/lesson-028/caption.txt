Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges matter because they directly affect NOI, tenant costs, and your underwriting. Reconciliation is the annual process comparing estimated monthly recoveries to actual expenses, producing credits or additional charges. Start by reading lease language: define recoverable expenses, set base year mechanics, limit capital recoveries, require audit rights, and specify dispute timelines. During due diligence request three years of CAM statements and vendor invoices, match line items to invoices, and separate operating from capital expenses.
