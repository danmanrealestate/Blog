Cash-on-cash return is a practical, cash-focused metric for income-minded commercial investors. Start by tallying all cash you put into the deal—down payment, closing fees, immediate rehab, and reserves—then divide annual pre-tax cash flow by that total. Use it to compare how quickly your equity produces cash, but remember it omits appreciation, tax benefits, and long-term returns. Always run multiple scenarios and stress-test vacancy, expense increases, and capital needs. For Chester and Montgomery County deals, add a local layer: estimate property tax and insurance variations and lean conservative on rent stability.
