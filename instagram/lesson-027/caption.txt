Lesson 27: Commercial Property Due Diligence Checklist — don’t skip the basics. Before you sign, run a structured checklist. Collect leases, rent rolls, operating statements, survey and title commitment. Schedule specialists to inspect roof, HVAC, structural elements and life-safety systems. Screen for environmental issues (Phase I ESA triggers), and confirm parking, ingress/egress, and municipal zoning allowances. Turn findings into decisions: quantify repair estimates, compare to contingency, and set firm red lines where you will walk or renegotiate. Assign deadlines and responsible parties so items are resolved before closing. For Chester and Montgomery County properties, expect older systems and differing municipal review workflows — allow time for permits and possible remediation.
