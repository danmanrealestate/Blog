Cash-on-cash return is a straightforward, deal-level metric that helps investors understand how much pre-tax cash a property returns each year relative to the cash they actually put into it. To calculate it, divide annual pre-tax cash flow by total initial cash invested. Include down payment, immediate repairs, and acquisition fees; exclude principal paydown, appreciation, and tax items. In Chester and Montgomery counties, local taxes, insurance, and operating costs influence the cash flow side, so run region-specific expense estimates during due diligence.
