Cash-on-cash return is one of the first metrics new commercial investors use to compare deals — and it’s especially practical for landlords evaluating near-term cash performance. At its core CoC = annual before-tax cash flow ÷ total cash invested, but that simple ratio hides important assumptions. Recompute CoC for different financing scenarios, include realistic reserves for capital expenditures and vacancy, and watch for one-off repairs or aggressive rent assumptions that can inflate short-term returns. Don’t stop at CoC: compare cap rate, IRR, and tax impacts to understand long-term wealth creation.
