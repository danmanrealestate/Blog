Lesson 38: Evaluating Tenant Quality and Lease Strength — a practical checklist for newer commercial investors and landlords in Chester and Montgomery Counties. Start by verifying the tenant’s legal entity and authorized signers. Then focus on lease durability: remaining term, renewal options, assignment limits, rent escalations, and guarantors matter as much as credit reports. Perform on-site operational checks, request basic financials and references, and flag short terms or generous termination/allowance language as urgent risks. Apply a holding-period rule: ensure lease coverage aligns with your exit timeline and local zoning or traffic patterns.
