Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is one of the first metrics underwriters and experienced investors use to judge whether a property’s cash flow can carry debt. Calculate DSCR as Net Operating Income divided by total annual debt service, and always use a consistent, evidence-backed NOI. When analyzing deals in Chester and Montgomery Counties, verify rent rolls, 12‑month P&Ls, and recent rent comps before relying on pro‑forma assumptions. Model debt service using amortizing payments rather than interest‑only shortfalls, and stress-test scenarios that reflect vacancy, concessions, or unexpected capex. Red flags include overly optimistic rent growth, excluded expenses, or a thin coverage cushion that disappears under modest stress. Collect documents, run conservative DSCR scenarios, and be prepared with contingency capital.
