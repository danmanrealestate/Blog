Rent rolls are the single-best snapshot of income and near-term risk for any commercial asset. Learning to read one lets you verify cash flow, uncover hidden concessions, and prioritize leasing work before you underwrite. Begin with a fast scan: tenant names, vacancy status, and lease start/end dates. Then verify those dates and reported rents against signed leases and bank deposit records. Check for clustered expirations, unexplained concessions, and whether CAM/utilities align with lease language. If you’re evaluating properties in Chester or Montgomery Counties, add an on-site walk and a quick local market check—block-level vacancy and tenant mix matter. Before you submit an offer, request signed leases, recent deposit records, and tenant contact lists so you can confirm income.
