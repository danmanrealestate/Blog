Lesson 19: How to evaluate a small industrial or flex property — practical steps for newer investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties. Start by remote-screening parcels: confirm zoning, roof age, clear height, and truck access with satellite imagery and tax records. Remember the core drivers: clear height, column spacing, loading, power capacity, and location relative to labor and transport. On-site, focus your inspection on dock types, turning radius, electrical one-line, HVAC/service history, and any environmental indicators like floor drains or stained concrete. Local takeaway: prioritize sites with light-industrial zoning and arterial access, and assemble parcel ID, floor plans, photos, and recent utility bills before asking for a feasibility review.
