Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges are one of the most misunderstood elements in commercial leases. CAMs reimburse shared operating costs—landscaping, parking lot repair, property insurance, utilities—and landlords collect estimates during the year then reconcile actual costs at year-end. For investors and landlords this affects net operating income and tenant relations, so understanding the mechanics matters. Use this carousel to identify CAM inclusions and exclusions in the lease, confirm the allocation formula and base year, check for caps or stop-loss protections, and insist on tenant audit rights and line-item invoices. During reconciliation, compare estimated CAM to actuals, verify credits or refunds are issued promptly, and track annual trends in a spreadsheet. Local note: properties in Chester and Montgomery Counties face winter maintenance and landscaping seasonality that can skew CAM budgets—request two years of reconciliations and vendor invoices before making offers.
