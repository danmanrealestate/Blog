Lesson 55: How to evaluate a small industrial or flex property — a practical guide for newer commercial investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties. Start with function: clear height, loading configuration, electrical capacity, and permitted uses determine which tenants fit and what cash flow is achievable. During due diligence prioritize truck access and circulation, municipal zoning and conditional uses, and mechanical systems like roof, HVAC, and fire protection. Always request service records, get contractor quotes, and confirm permitted uses with code enforcement. Build realistic capital expenditure reserves and factor potential downtime for tenant turnover. Common red flags: mismatched tenant use versus zoning, inadequate dock access, undersized electrical service, and deferred roof or HVAC maintenance.
