Lesson 43: How to evaluate a small industrial or flex property — a practical checklist for newer investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties. Start with a methodical walkaround: photograph rooflines, docks, utilities, and drainage. Prioritize core metrics — clear height, usable bay area, power capacity, and loading configuration — because adaptability drives tenant demand. Check truck turning radii, pavement, and staging to avoid access-related vacancy. Open mechanical rooms to verify HVAC and electrical that could become early capital costs. Confirm zoning, conditional uses, and past property uses to surface environmental or permitting red flags. Locally, reach out to county planning or municipal zoning officers early: truck restrictions, stormwater rules, and permitted uses vary town-by-town and affect underwriting.
