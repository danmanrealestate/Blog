Lesson 43 — How to evaluate a small industrial or flex property in Chester & Montgomery Counties. Start by matching likely tenant use to the building’s layout, clear height, and power capacity. Verify zoning and permitted uses with the township before you underwrite. Order inspections for roof, HVAC, electrical systems and get maintenance records — deferred systems become negotiation points. Physically test truck access and parking; curb cuts and turning radii matter. Factor county permitting timelines, utility capacity, and stormwater rules into your schedule and offer. If leasing or permit hurdles will materially delay occupancy, adjust price or walk. Want a local checklist to use on-site?
