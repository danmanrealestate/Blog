Triple Net (NNN) leases can simplify landlord responsibilities and stabilize cash flow — but they also transfer many costs and potential surprises to tenants. For newer investors, landlords, and business owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties, the critical steps are straightforward: read definitions of taxes, insurance, and CAM carefully; insist on audit rights and caps; confirm who covers structural and capital repairs; and verify tenant credit and assignability. Older local building stock often needs explicit capital repair clauses. Don’t sign until a local commercial attorney and an experienced advisor have reviewed key clauses.
