Lesson 15: Commercial Property Due Diligence Checklist — designed for commercial real estate investors, landlords, building owners, and business owners in Chester County and Montgomery County, Pennsylvania. This lesson outlines the inspections, reports, and verifications to complete before you close: title and survey review, zoning and permit confirmation, environmental screening, building systems inspections, tenant and lease audits, and an itemized estimate of near-term expenses. Following a standardized checklist reduces surprises, clarifies budget, preserves value, and improves negotiating leverage.
