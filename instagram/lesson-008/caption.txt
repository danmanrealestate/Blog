A solid rent roll review is one of the fastest ways to understand a commercial asset: who’s paying, for how long, and how reliably. Start by verifying tenant names, lease dates, and the rent frequency. Watch for short-term leases, large tenants that dominate revenue, and vacancy timing. Drill into lease type (NNN vs gross) and any expense recoveries—these change the landlord’s net cash flow. Cross-check concessions, free rent periods, and start/end dates against the signed leases. Don’t rely solely on totals: vacancy, market rent, and upcoming expirations drive risk. If you own or evaluate properties in Chester or Montgomery Counties, use your rent roll to prioritize inspections, lease document requests, and tenant credit checks.
