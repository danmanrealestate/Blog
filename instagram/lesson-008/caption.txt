A clear rent roll is one of the fastest ways to understand a commercial asset. It organizes tenant names, lease dates, square footage, and income sources so you can verify reported cash flow and identify upcoming risks like clustered expirations or undocumented concessions. When reviewing a rent roll, check tenant identity, lease term certainty, rent payment frequency, expense pass-throughs, and any noted security deposits or credits. For investors, landlords, and building owners in Chester and Montgomery Counties, a reliable rent roll speeds underwriting, supports negotiations, and guides leasing strategy.
